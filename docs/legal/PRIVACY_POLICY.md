# Who we are and how to contact us

Komprexo helps you compress, resize and convert images on Android. Developer legal name and business address: OWNER ACTION REQUIRED. Send privacy questions to komprexo.support@gmail.com.

# Images you select

Komprexo accesses images you choose and information needed to read them, such as format, dimensions and orientation. Temporary copies may include camera metadata from the original. Processing and previews happen on your device. Processing does not require uploading photos to a Komprexo server. Komprexo has no photo-processing server or app account. If you choose an image from an online provider, that provider may need a connection to supply it and applies its own policy.

# Temporary files, Save and Share

The app keeps temporary input, output and preview files in private app storage. Originals are not changed. Some temporary files are removed when work is replaced or cleared, or a process fails. Files older than 24 hours are checked for cleanup when image storage is initialized; deletion is not guaranteed exactly after 24 hours. Save important results. Saving writes a copy to your chosen destination. Sharing gives your chosen app access to a result. A cloud storage provider or receiving app may upload or retain that copy under its own policy. Komprexo cannot retract exported copies.

# Local quotas and preferences

The app keeps daily feature counts, information about unfinished operations and your language preference locally. These records are not an image history. Counts reset according to the daily quota rules. Preferences remain until changed or removed. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes private app files and records; it does not delete exported files or Google purchase records. App backup is disabled.

# Purchases and Google services

Google Play handles checkout and payment details. Komprexo uses purchase information to activate, restore and check Premium. It does not store card details or a purchase-order history. A protected local ownership record supports up to 24 hours of offline Premium after a successful check. Expired or invalid records are removed when next checked. Purchases and renewed ownership checks need internet. Image processing itself works offline.

# Third-party diagnostics

Google Play and its included software may process purchase, account, device, network and diagnostic information under their policies. Billing-related software may store and send service diagnostics. The precise data fields, retention and deletion arrangements still need confirmation before publication: OWNER ACTION REQUIRED. No claim of zero data collection is made. Komprexo has no separate app analytics or advertising service. It does not request device location permission or call location services. That does not establish what Google may infer from network information.

# Retention and security

Local temporary files, quota records and preferences follow the rules above. The offline Premium record is valid for at most 24 hours; this is not a deletion deadline for Google records or diagnostics from included Google software. Google and any storage, sharing or email provider have their own retention rules. Private storage and a protected ownership record reduce risk, but do not guarantee absolute security or encrypt every photo. Device compromise and delayed offline refund checks remain possible.

You can choose which images to process, revoke access, change language, clear app data and delete copies you saved. Revoking permission does not delete a copy already imported.
