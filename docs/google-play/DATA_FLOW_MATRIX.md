Status vocabulary:

- **FULLY VERIFIED**: narrow inspectable fact such as resolved version/manifest/hash or passing fixture test; not entire app/vendor privacy compliance.
- **SUPPORTED BY SOURCE-CODE INSPECTION**: implementation/code path, not physical-device network observation.
- **VENDOR DOCUMENTED**: official generic or specific vendor statement; scope explicitly stated.
- **REQUIRES RUNTIME VERIFICATION**: fields/events/timing/device/provider behavior not observed.
- **REQUIRES OWNER CONFIRMATION**: actual Console/mailbox/market/identity operation.
- **UNKNOWN**: no evidence for the asserted field/role/deadline/classification.
