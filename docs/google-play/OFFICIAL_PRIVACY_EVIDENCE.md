## Supported markets are not established by translation languages

Indonesia is the operator country and intended IDR price country. Five UI languages do not prove launch eligibility/regions or all countries where users will be located. Owner must inspect actual Play country availability and required account/merchant/regional public disclosures; qualified advisers must assess Indonesian and any applicable consumer/privacy/child/cross-border laws in actual launch markets. Google G12 explicitly warns of regional additional public details. No statement that every market's laws are satisfied, no invented governing court and no blanket exemption. Personal-contact minimization in source cannot override legally required public disclosures.
