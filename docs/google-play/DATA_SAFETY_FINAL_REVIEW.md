## Definitions and scoped conclusions

Play collection concerns user data transmitted off device, including SDK transmissions. Purely on-device photos/quotas are outside collection scope. Sharing can include transfer to another app; a expected user-initiated transfer or service-provider exception may apply only when its conditions are supported. Being Google-owned is not proof of a service-provider exemption. Ephemeral transmission still needs assessment; hashed/pseudonymous identifiers are not automatically anonymous. Narrow provider-direct card-data exception is distinct from app-accessible purchase ownership/history/token data.

Review sum of release functionality across all users/regions/configurations. Buying Premium is optional, but Billing initialization/query/SDK logger paths occur at launch/foreground for Free users too. No user-controlled SDK diagnostic opt-out has been verified. Do not mark all relevant data optional because checkout is optional. Source/bytecode shows potential events/metadata/queue; no actual packet capture or real Google Play transaction confirms exact sent data. No globally qualified No collection/No sharing or all-traffic encrypted answer is approved.
